A photorealistic, interactable 3D scene reconstruction pipeline based on 3D Gaussian Splatting, extended to dynamic articulated scenes via disentangled background/object Gaussians and RANSAC-based mobility fitting. Presented as a lightning talk and poster. Developed as my Master's thesis at the CVG Lab, ETH Zürich.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24628)

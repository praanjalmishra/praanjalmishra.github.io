👋🏼 Hi, I'm Pranjal, a robotics software engineer focused on 3D perception, state estimation, and real-time control. I build multi-sensor pipelines (stereo, RGB-D, LiDAR, IMU) for pose estimation, 3D reconstruction, and sensor fusion that run in real time on embedded hardware, using C++, Python, and ROS 2. My work covers the full path from sensor calibration to perception-to-control integration, taking systems from prototype to reliable operation on real robots.

I completed my MSc in [Computational Science & Engineering](https://rw.ethz.ch/) (Robotics & Computer Vision) at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where I also worked as a Robotics Engineer at the [SCAI Lab](https://scai.ethz.ch/) on assistive robotics and multi-view 3D reconstruction. Before that, I studied at [IIT Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/) and spent two years in R&D at [Sterlite Technologies](https://www.stl.tech/) building simulation models for industrial processes.

**I'm currently open to robotics / perception engineering roles and available to start immediately.** Here's my [CV](/files/cv_pranjal_ETHz.pdf).

Recent work: [ArtiTwinSplat](/publication/2026-06-01-artitwinsplat) (interactable digital twins with Gaussian Splatting, ICRA 2026 workshop) and [ROS 4 Healthcare](/publication/2026-01-01-ros4healthcare) (*Frontiers in Robotics and AI*). See all [projects](/portfolio/).
